# 14 · Conversion Strategy

The funnel truth for a Nagpur studio: **conversion is a conversation.** No one buys a membership on a website here — they start a WhatsApp chat, make a call, or book a trial. The site's job is to manufacture enough trust and desire that starting that conversation feels easy and obvious, then to hand a warm lead to a human who closes.

## Conversion hierarchy

1. **Primary: WhatsApp click-to-chat** — `wa.me/<number>?text=…` with context-aware pre-filled messages:
   - Global/sticky: "Hi! I'd like to book a trial class at 2Stepz 💃"
   - Zumba page: "Hi! I'm interested in the Zumba batches — can you share timings & a trial?"
   - Membership: "Hi! Please share current membership offers."
   - Schedule rows: "Hi! Is there space in the {day} {time} {format} batch?"
   Pre-filled text ≈ zero typing, and it tells the studio the lead's intent instantly. Use **WhatsApp Business** (catalog, quick replies, away messages) — verify the number is registered.
2. **Secondary: tap-to-call** — shown with live open/closed state ("Open now · till 8 pm"); calling a closed number is a lost lead, so after hours the bar promotes WhatsApp ("We'll reply first thing tomorrow").
3. **Tertiary: 3-field trial form** — for the shy and the after-hours browser; delivers to WhatsApp/email; promise a reply window ("within a few hours, no spam ever").

## Sticky CTA system

- Mobile: bottom bar (WhatsApp · Call · **Book Trial**) per §11 behavior rules. One bar, site-wide, always the same three actions — consistency builds muscle memory.
- Desktop: header "Book a Free Trial" button + floating WhatsApp bubble bottom-right.
- Every page terminates in a full-width CTA band: proof line + button ("4.6★ from 88+ members — your first class is the easiest step").

## Trust indicators (layered, per §08 principle "proof before ask")

- **Numbers:** 4.6★ rating · since 2015 · formats count · members/classes-taught count (get real figures from owner).
- **Authority:** Mrs Fit India 2022 badge; press logos linking to real articles.
- **Social proof:** named reviews with sources; video testimonials; transformation stories with timeframes; live Instagram feed (a feed updated yesterday proves the studio is alive today).
- **Google review integration:** display genuine Google/Justdial snippets with a "see all reviews" link out (linking *out* to reviews is a confidence move that itself builds trust) and a "review us" link for members — the flywheel that feeds §06's local-SEO engine.
- **Practical trust:** full address + map + landmark photo, real schedule, named instructor, photographed interior — each removes one "is this real?" doubt.

## Urgency & scarcity — the honest kind only

- **Real batch capacity:** "Morning batch: 3 spots left" — only if actually tracked; fake counters destroy exactly the trust this brand runs on.
- **Seasonal deadlines:** "Navratri Power Garba batch starts Sept 14 — enrolment closes Sept 10." Festival calendars create genuine, recurring urgency.
- **Offer windows:** festival pricing with visible end dates.
- Banned: countdown timers that reset, "17 people are viewing," fake "today only."

## Lead generation & nurture

- **Lead magnet (soft):** "Get the batch timetable + this month's offer on WhatsApp" — captures the not-ready-yet visitor into a channel the studio owns.
- **Bring-a-friend mechanic:** trial confirmation includes "bring a friend — both classes free"; matches the real decision unit (§08) and halves acquisition cost.
- **Referral loop for members:** a /refer page or WhatsApp template ("gift a free class") — word of mouth is already the studio's engine; give it a link.
- **Corporate funnel:** separate enquiry form + a one-page PDF the site offers ("Workplace wellness by 2Stepz") — higher-value, longer-cycle leads kept out of the consumer funnel.
- **Follow-up discipline (offsite but decisive):** every web lead gets a WhatsApp response same-day; a simple three-touch sequence (reply → reminder day before trial → post-trial "how was it? here's the joining offer + review link"). The website can only lose leads slower than the studio answers them.

## Measurement & iteration

- GA4 events on: WhatsApp taps (by source page), calls, form submits, schedule views, /trial visits. UTM discipline on GBP, Instagram bio, QR codes, ads.
- North-star metric: **conversations started per week** (WhatsApp + calls + forms), not pageviews.
- 90-day test queue (one at a time): hero headline variants (§10) → trial framing ("free" vs "₹99") → pricing shown vs "from ₹—" → sticky-bar label ("Book Trial" vs "Try a Class Free").
- Review velocity check monthly: Google review count and rating vs the Justdial baseline (4.6/88).

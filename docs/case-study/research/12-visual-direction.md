# 12 · Visual Direction

The brief from §07: **celebration, crafted.** Joyful energy held inside boutique-premium restraint, with an unmistakably Indian warmth. Below is direction, not final design — a designer executes; these are the guardrails.

## Color palette

Strategy: a disciplined neutral foundation (the "premium" layer) + one hot signature accent (the "dance" layer) + festival-capable support colors.

| Role | Direction | Rationale |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Foundation | Warm off-white / cream (#FAF7F2-zone) for light surfaces; deep charcoal-plum (#221B26-zone) for dark sections | Warm neutrals read boutique (Aesop lesson) without Equinox coldness; a plum-tinted dark is feminine-strong, not gym-black |
| **Signature accent** | A confident magenta-pink → deep rani-pink (#D91A6D-zone) | Owns "dance + celebration + women-first" in one hue; rani pink is culturally resonant, high-energy, and unclaimed by any local competitor (Cult = black/red, big gyms = black/yellow/red) |
| Secondary accent | Marigold/amber (#F2A93B-zone), used sparingly | Festival warmth (Garba!), pairs naturally with rani pink; reserved for highlights, offers, event pages |
| Functional | Success/error greens/reds kept muted and standard | Utility, not brand |

Rules: accent on ≤ 10 % of any screen (CTAs, key words, live details); long sections alternate light/dark rhythmically; WCAG 4.5:1 contrast enforced (the pink must be dark enough on cream — test).

## Typography

- **Display/headlines:** a high-contrast, confident face with warmth — e.g., a modern grotesk with personality or a display serif for editorial moments. Direction: *big, tight, assured* (Apple/Equinox lesson: typography is where "premium" lives). Devanagari-compatible family strongly preferred so occasional Hindi words ("नमस्ते," campaign lines) don't break the system.
- **Body/UI:** a clean humanist sans with excellent Devanagari + Latin support (the Google-Fonts tier makes this free: e.g., a well-hinted variable sans). 17–18 px body on mobile.
- Scale: large jumps (e.g., 1.333 ratio+) — headlines should feel 3–4× body, not 1.5×. Two families max.

## Photography direction (the single biggest visual investment)

- **Real people, real studio, real sweat, real laughter.** Zero stock. The research is unanimous: authenticity is the premium signal (Equinox/Barry's shoot their own floors; reviews praise *people*, not machines).
- Shot list for a half-day shoot: classes mid-motion (slow-shutter energy blur for hero use), founder portraits (both powerful and warm), member close-ups laughing (with releases), studio details (mirrors, props, dandiya sticks, TRX straps), the building entrance/landmark (wayfinding + Contact page), event/garba wide shots with crowd energy.
- Grade: warm, slightly saturated, golden-hour bias — celebration, not the desaturated teal of generic gym sites.
- Aspect discipline: shoot verticals deliberately (9:16 heroes for mobile) — not crops of horizontals.

## Iconography & shape language

- Icons: rounded-line style, consistent 1.5–2 px stroke, no filled cartoon icons — friendly but adult.
- Shape language: **rounded-but-not-bubbly** (12–16 px radii on cards/buttons); occasional circular motifs echoing garba's circular dance and mirror-work — a subtle, ownable geometry (e.g., a ring/step motif in section dividers or the logo refresh).
- Photography frames may break the grid slightly (tilted polaroid-style in Gallery) to carry energy — everywhere else, the grid is sacred.

## Spacing & visual rhythm

- Generous whitespace is the cheapest luxury: 80–120 px section padding on mobile, more on desktop.
- Rhythm pattern per §08: *proof → feeling → ask*, visually expressed as alternating photo-led and type-led sections; never two dense sections adjacent.
- One idea per viewport (Apple lesson). If a section needs a paragraph AND six bullets AND a grid, it's two sections.

## Illustration & graphic language

- Minimal illustration; the brand is photographic. Permitted graphic devices: oversized outlined numerals (stats), accent-colored underline strokes on key words (hand-drawn energy, used ≤ once per screen), subtle mirror-work/dot pattern as a texture on dark sections at low opacity.
- No gradients-on-everything, no glassmorphism, no neon glows.

## Micro-interactions (visual layer; motion rules in §13)

- Buttons: slight lift + accent deepen on press; WhatsApp button in its brand green only in the sticky bar (recognition > palette purity there).
- Cards: 2–4 px rise on tap/hover.
- Review stars draw in once on first view; stats count up once.

## Emotional tone check

Every screen should pass: *Would a 34-year-old woman in Dharampeth screenshot this to her friend with "yeh dekho, join karein?"* If it looks like a bank (too cold) or a mela poster (too loud), recalibrate toward the middle: **warm, confident, alive.**

# 09 · Information Architecture

## Site map

```
Home
├── About  (studio story + Madhumita + press)
├── Classes  (hub)
│   ├── Zumba  (incl. Toning, Strong by Zumba)
│   ├── Power Garba & Folk Fitness
│   ├── Bokwa
│   ├── Pilates & Yoga
│   ├── TRX & Functional Training
│   ├── Kids' Programs
│   └── Corporate & School Workshops
├── Schedule
├── Membership & Pricing
├── Trial Booking  (/trial — the conversion page)
├── Transformations & Testimonials
├── Events & Gallery
├── Blog
├── FAQ
└── Contact
```

Primary nav (mobile): Classes · Schedule · Membership · About · Contact, plus an always-visible **Book Trial** button. Everything else lives in footer + contextual links. (Careers: not warranted at this scale — a single line on Contact, "Instructors: write to us," suffices.)

## Page-by-page specification

### Home
- **Purpose:** identity + trust + funnel entry. **Audience:** first-time local searchers and Instagram click-throughs. **Conversion objective:** trial booking (WhatsApp).
- **Sections:** hero (motion + headline + CTA) → trust strip (4.6★ · since 2015 · Mrs Fit India 2022 · 10+ formats) → format cards → founder intro → reviews carousel → transformation teaser → events/community strip → Instagram feed → first-class explainer → location/map + hours → final CTA band.
- **CTAs:** primary "Book a Free Trial"; secondary "See the Schedule"; sticky bar (WhatsApp/Call/Trial).
- **Required content:** hero video/photos from a real class; 4 headline stats; 6 format blurbs; 3 best reviews; founder portrait + 3 lines.

### About
- **Purpose:** the deep-trust page — founder story, studio story, credentials, press. **Audience:** considered deciders, parents, corporates. **Objective:** belief → trial or enquiry.
- **Sections:** the 2015 origin story → Madhumita profile (titles, philosophy, one human paragraph) → milestones timeline (founding, masterclasses, anniversaries, titles) → press logos/links (The Live Nagpur, Nagpur Today) → team/other instructors (if any) → values ("no judgment, all levels") → CTA.

### Classes (hub)
- **Purpose:** router + SEO hub for "fitness classes nagpur." **Objective:** route to a format page in one tap.
- **Sections:** intro line, card grid (photo, one-line feeling, "for you if…" chip), comparison helper ("Not sure? Take the trial and sample formats"), CTA.

### Each format page (Zumba / Power Garba / Bokwa / Pilates & Yoga / TRX / Kids / Corporate)
- **Purpose:** the SEO landing pages (§06) and the "is this for me?" answer. **Audience:** format-intent searchers. **Objective:** trial booking for that format.
- **Sections:** hero photo of *that* class → what it is (2 short paras, keyword-natural) → who it's for / results to expect → batch timings (pulled from schedule) → instructor → 2 format-specific reviews → mini-FAQ (3–5 questions, FAQ schema) → CTA with pre-filled WhatsApp text naming the format.
- **Corporate page differs:** case blurbs of past school/corporate events, offer structure, enquiry form instead of trial CTA.

### Schedule
- **Purpose:** the most-revisited utility page. **Objective:** reduce "what time?" calls; feed trial bookings.
- **Sections:** week grid (day × time × format × instructor), filter chips by format, "batch full?" honesty notes, screenshot-friendly layout, per-row "Book this batch" WhatsApp link. Must be effortless for the owner to update (CMS or a structured data file — not baked into design).

### Membership & Pricing
- **Purpose:** qualify and reassure; convert fence-sitters. **Objective:** membership enquiry.
- **Sections:** outcome-first intro ("what members get") → tier cards (monthly/quarterly/annual; kids; personal training) — with real prices if owner agrees, else "from ₹X" anchors + "get current offer on WhatsApp" → what's included → trial-first reassurance ("start with a free class — decide later") → offers/festival deals slot → payment logistics (UPI etc.) → FAQ (freeze policy, refunds) → CTA.

### Trial Booking (/trial)
- **Purpose:** the single conversion URL every ad, bio link, QR, and GBP button points to. **Objective:** a WhatsApp conversation or submitted form.
- **Sections:** promise ("First class on us / ₹X, no strings") → 3-field form (name, phone, preferred batch) → or one-tap WhatsApp → what happens next (we confirm on WhatsApp, come 10 min early, wear comfy shoes) → micro-trust row (rating, since 2015) → location pin.

### Transformations & Testimonials
- **Purpose:** the belief engine. **Objective:** move "will it work for *me*?" to yes.
- **Sections:** 4–8 member stories (photo, first name, goal, timeframe, quote — with written consent), video testimonials if available, wall of review snippets (Google/Justdial, linked), "your story starts with one class" CTA.

### Events & Gallery
- **Purpose:** show the community 2Stepz uniquely has. **Objective:** emotional conviction; secondary: event sign-ups.
- **Sections:** upcoming events/workshops (Event schema), past-event albums (Garba nights, anniversaries, masterclasses with captions and dates), press mentions, CTA.

### Blog
- **Purpose:** SEO compounding + objection handling (calendar in §06). Each post: one question, one format-page link, one trial CTA.

### FAQ
- **Purpose:** kill the anxieties (§03 list) and the logistics questions in one indexed place; FAQPage schema.
- **Content:** beginner fears, ladies-only batches, age limits, what to wear, trial policy, fees process, parking/stairs honesty, kids' batches, corporate enquiries.

### Contact
- **Purpose:** every path to the door. **Sections:** tap-to-call, WhatsApp, address with embedded map + landmark photo ("2nd floor, Gubre Bhavan — look for us above…"), hours, Instagram/Facebook links, short message form. NAP here must match GBP exactly.

## Global elements
- **Header:** logo, 5-item nav, Book Trial button.
- **Sticky mobile bar:** WhatsApp · Call · Book Trial (see §14 for behavior rules).
- **Footer:** NAP + hours, format-page links (SEO), social links, review link, mini-map, "Since 2015" mark.

# 10 · Content Strategy

Principles first, then page-level direction. All copy recommendations trace to research findings: the review vocabulary (fun, energy, enthusiasm, creativity), the founder's verified credentials, and the anxieties of the first-time visitor.

## Voice & tone rules

1. **Write in the customers' own emotional vocabulary.** Reviews say *fun, energy, enjoyable* — so the site says "the workout you'll actually look forward to," never "crush your goals" or "no pain no gain."
2. **Warm Hinglish-friendly English.** Simple sentences; the occasional Hindi phrase where it adds warmth ("Chalo, pehla step le lo") — matching how the audience actually talks on WhatsApp.
3. **Second person, present tense.** "You walk in nervous. You leave laughing." The visitor is the hero; Madhumita is the guide (the Nike pattern).
4. **Every claim carries its proof.** "A decade of classes" sits beside "Since 2015"; "loved by members" sits beside a named review.
5. **Honesty as a feature.** Batch full? Say so. Second floor, stairs? Say so, with a smile. Honesty is a premium signal directories can't fake.

## Page-level content direction

### Home
- **Headline territory** (pick via testing, all grounded in review language):
  - "The best hour of your day."
  - "Fitness that feels like a celebration."
  - "Dance first. Fitness follows."
- Subline: "Nagpur's boutique dance-fitness studio — Zumba, Power Garba, Bokwa & more, led by Mrs Fit India 2022. Since 2015."
- Trust strip copy: `★ 4.6 rated · 10+ years in Gokulpeth · Mrs Fit India 2022 · 10+ class formats` *(re-verify numbers at launch)*.
- Format cards: one feeling per line — Zumba: "Latin beats, zero judgment." Power Garba: "Navratri energy, all year." Bokwa: "Draw the alphabet. Break a sweat." Pilates: "Strong core, calm mind."
- First-class explainer: 4 steps with icons — Message us → Pick your batch → Wear anything comfy → We'll take care of the rest.

### About
- Origin story arc (verify details with owner): 2015, one instructor and a dream on Canal Road → formats added year by year → masterclasses with national names (Satyajit Vora) → Mrs India Multimedia 2017 → Mrs Fit India 2022 → today: a community, not a gym.
- Madhumita bio: titles as facts, but the paragraph is human — why she teaches, what she believes about women and fitness. End on her voice, a one-line pull quote.
- Press section: link the actual The Live Nagpur / Nagpur Today articles — third-party ink is rare among competitors.

### Format pages
- Structure each as: *the feeling → the facts → the fit* ("You'll love this if…") → timings → proof → CTA.
- Power Garba page deserves the richest treatment (unique inventory): heritage angle ("India's favorite dance, engineered into a workout"), the Satyajit Vora masterclass story, Navratri-batch seasonal block.
- Kids page speaks to parents: safety, confidence, screen-time antidote, batch timings after school.
- Corporate page speaks ROI-lite: energy, team bonding, done-for-you events; cite past school/college/corporate engagements (verified in press) without naming clients until permission.

### Membership & Pricing
- Lead with belonging, not tiers: "Membership isn't a fee here. It's a seat in the circle."
- Tier cards named simply (Monthly / Quarterly / Annual / Kids / Personal Training). If owner withholds prices: use "from ₹—" anchors and a same-screen WhatsApp CTA "Get today's offer" — never a bare "Contact us" wall.
- Reassurance row: pause policy, no lock-in pressure, trial-first.

### Transformations
- Story template per member (collect via owner, with written consent): name, age optional, "before" feeling (not necessarily photo), what changed, timeframe, quote. Feelings-based transformation ("I stopped dreading Mondays") is as powerful as weight numbers and easier to consent.
- Aim for range: weight loss, confidence, post-pregnancy, 50+, the shy beginner.

### FAQ (the anxiety-killers, drafted)
- "I've never danced / I have two left feet." → "Perfect. Half the room started that way. No steps are 'wrong' here."
- "Am I too old / unfit to start?" → members' age range + start-slow promise.
- "Is it ladies-only?" → answer per actual batch structure (verify).
- "What should I wear?" · "Do I need to book?" · "Fees?" (route to WhatsApp) · "Parking / finding the studio?" (honest, with landmark photo).

### Blog
- Calendar in §06. Format: 700–1,200 words, one H1 question, scannable H2s, real studio photos, one internal link to a format page, closing trial CTA. Author byline: Madhumita (E-E-A-T: a titled expert authoring fitness content is a genuine authority signal).

## Content the owner must supply (collection checklist)
1. Professional photo/video half-day shoot: classes in motion, founder portraits, studio details, laughing members (release forms!).
2. 5–8 member testimonials (text) + 2–3 video testimonials, with consent.
3. Current schedule, pricing decisions, trial policy.
4. Milestone dates/photos for the About timeline; award photos.
5. WhatsApp Business number + agreed auto-reply templates.

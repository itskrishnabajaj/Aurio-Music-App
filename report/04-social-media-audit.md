## What the content record shows

- **Reels with personality:** the "We at 46° heat…" reel (May 2024) shows the studio's voice — playful defiance, community in motion, real sweat. This is exactly the right register.
- **Event-driven content:** Power Garba masterclass with format founder Satyajit Vora, anniversary bashes with press coverage, festival programming. The studio generates *occasions*, which most gyms cannot.
- **Founder as protagonist:** across both handles, Madhumita is the face — consistent with reviews that name her. Brand and founder accounts cross-promote.
- **~510 posts over ~10 years** ≈ one post a week — consistent, but volume alone hasn't translated into reach (1.6K followers).

## Strengths

1. **Authenticity** — real members, real classes, real events; nothing stocky or fake.
2. **A charismatic lead** the camera already loves; press-validated.
3. **Occasion engine** — Navratri/Garba, anniversaries, masterclasses give the calendar natural spikes.
4. **Name consistency** — @2stepzfitness matches the Facebook handle; findable.

## Weaknesses

1. **Reach ≪ reputation.** ~1.6K followers after a decade means content is made for existing members, not for discovery. Reels likely lack the hooks/keywords/covers that travel.
2. **No link infrastructure.** With no website, the bio link (if any) can only point to WhatsApp or a linktree — every campaign dead-ends.
3. **Two-handle dilution.** Studio and founder accounts split an already small audience; roles should be defined (studio = classes/schedule/offers; founder = authority/press/personal journey) with deliberate cross-posting.
4. **YouTube is unclaimed ground.** Zumba choreo videos are highly searched in India; a lightly-run channel leaves free discovery on the table.
5. **No transformation/testimonial format.** The most convincing fitness content genre — member stories — isn't a visible recurring series.
6. **Brand-name collision.** Search for "stepz fitness" surfaces Australian chains and other studios; without a website and strong local signals, 2Stepz cedes its own brand SERP.

## Brand voice (observed)

Warm, high-energy, festive, inclusive, Hinglish-friendly, proud of Indian dance culture, celebration-forward. The voice is right; it needs **packaging and distribution**, not reinvention.

## What the website should do with social

- **Ingest, don't duplicate:** an Instagram-feed strip on the homepage (latest reels) keeps the site alive without a content team.
- **Be the destination:** every reel caption, every bio, every GBP post links to one place — the site's trial-booking page.
- **Canonize the best:** the site's Gallery and Events pages should preserve the greatest hits (Garba nights, anniversaries, 46°-heat energy) that otherwise sink down the feed.
- **Elevate the founder page:** consolidate titles, press links, and story on an About/Founder page — the durable home for authority that Instagram bios can't hold.

## Content plays to recommend to the owner (post-launch)

1. **Weekly member-story reel** ("I joined because… / what changed…") — feeds the site's Transformations page.
2. **Choreo snippets to YouTube Shorts + Instagram** with song-name keywords (how Zumba content gets discovered in India).
3. **Festival campaigns** — "Navratri-ready in 6 weeks: Power Garba batch" is a uniquely ownable seasonal funnel.
4. **Behind-the-batch** — timings, what to bring, first-class walkthrough — content that doubles as objection-handling for the nervous beginner.

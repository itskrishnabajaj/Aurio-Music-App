## Services offered (verified via listings and press)

**Dance fitness (the core):** Zumba, Zumba Toning, Strong by Zumba, **Power Garba** (incl. a masterclass hosted with format founder Satyajit Vora), **Bokwa**, folk fitness.

**Mind-body & strength:** Pilates, yoga, meditation, functional training, TRX, cross-fit-style conditioning.

**Beyond classes:** kids' workshops, ladies' workshops, grooming sessions, beauty-pageant audition prep, corporate and school/college guest lectures and fitness events (conducted in Nagpur and other cities).

This breadth matters: **no single Nagpur competitor offers this mix.** Cult.fit offers dance fitness as one option among many; local Zumba studios offer Zumba alone. Only 2Stepz spans Latin (Zumba), African (Bokwa), and Indian folk (Power Garba, folk fitness) dance formats plus mind-body work under one roof.

## Membership model

Public sources do not list pricing or membership tiers — typical for Indian boutique studios, where pricing is shared on enquiry (phone/WhatsApp/walk-in). **Flag for owner interview:** monthly/quarterly/annual tiers, per-class drop-in rates, trial policy, kids' batch pricing, corporate packages. The website should present the *structure* of membership clearly even if exact prices remain "enquire for current offer" (see Conversion Strategy for the trade-offs).

## Customer demographics & target audience

Evidence (review language, class mix, workshop types, "ladies' workshops," pageant grooming, women-only competitor patterns in Nagpur) points to:

- **Primary:** Women 25–50 in West Nagpur (Gokulpeth, Dharampeth, Ramdaspeth, Civil Lines) — homemakers and working professionals seeking fun, social, judgment-free fitness; weight-loss and toning goals expressed as "I want to enjoy working out."
- **Secondary:** Kids (workshop batches), teens (dance + fitness), brides-to-be and pageant aspirants (grooming), and **corporates/schools** buying events and lectures.
- **Tertiary:** Festival-season demand spikes — Power Garba before Navratri is a uniquely Indian acquisition moment competitors ignore.

## Brand personality (as it exists today)

Energetic, warm, festive, feminine-strong, community-first, founder-led. The 46 °C-heat workout reel, anniversary bashes, and Garba events signal a brand that treats fitness as **celebration, not punishment**. This is the personality the website must amplify — not replace with generic gym toughness.

## Strengths

1. **Founder equity** — a titled, press-covered, camera-ready personality. Most gyms have to invent a brand; 2Stepz has one walking around teaching classes.
2. **A decade of survival** — est. 2015; longevity itself is trust in a market where studios open and close in 18 months.
3. **Format breadth & exclusivity** — Power Garba and Bokwa are near-unique in Nagpur.
4. **Strong, improving public rating** — 4.5→4.6 with rising review counts.
5. **Events culture** — anniversaries, masterclasses, festival events = a stream of content and community proof.
6. **Location** — Gokulpeth/near-Times-of-India is central to affluent West Nagpur.

## Weaknesses

1. **No website** — zero owned digital real estate; can't rank, can't convert, can't control the narrative.
2. **Small social following** (~1.6K) relative to a 10-year history — content exists but isn't engineered for reach.
3. **Founder-dependence** — the brand *is* Madhumita; other trainers (if any) are invisible publicly. Scaling and continuity risk.
4. **No visible pricing, schedule, or booking path** online — every prospect must call or DM cold.
5. **Review concentration on Justdial** rather than Google — Justdial reviews don't move Google Maps rankings.
6. **Discovery fragility** — brand name "2Stepz" is easily confused with unrelated "Stepz Fitness" brands (Australia) and "Stepz Dance Fitness Studio" — search results already interleave them. Owning `2stepz` domains and consistent NAP (name-address-phone) is urgent.

## Unique selling points (for the website to lead with)

1. **"Fitness with Madhumita"** — train with Mrs Fit India 2022, in person, in Nagpur.
2. **India's dances, world's formats** — Zumba to Power Garba to Bokwa in one studio.
3. **10+ years, 4.6★** — the longest-running boutique dance-fitness studio in the city.
4. **Women-first, all-welcome** — a safe, social, joyful space (a message big-box gyms cannot credibly send).
5. **More than a gym** — workshops, grooming, events, corporate programs: a movement community.

## Open questions for the owner (fill before site copy is written)

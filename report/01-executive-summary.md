# 01 · Executive Summary

## The situation

2Stepz Fitness & Dance Studio has spent a decade (established 2015) building something most gyms never achieve: a **community with a face**. Founder Madhumita Gubre is not just an owner — she is the product. She is a nationally titled fitness personality (Mrs India Multimedia 2017, Fitness Influencer 2018, **Mrs Fit India 2022**), the lead instructor, and the reason reviews consistently mention *energy, enthusiasm, and creativity*. The studio holds a **4.6/5 rating across ~88 public reviews** on Justdial and offers the widest dance-fitness format range in Nagpur: Zumba, Zumba Toning, Strong by Zumba, Power Garba, Bokwa, folk fitness, Pilates, yoga, meditation, functional training, and TRX.

And yet, in 2026, **2Stepz has no website**. Its digital identity is rented: a Justdial listing it doesn't control, an Instagram account (~1,658 followers) fighting the algorithm, and a Facebook page. When someone in Nagpur searches "Zumba classes near me," the studio's fate is decided by third-party directories and a Google Business Profile that (based on all public evidence) is under-optimized.

## The core insight

2Stepz's competition splits into two camps, and 2Stepz fits neither:

1. **Big-box chains** (Cult.fit Dharampeth, MultiFit, Gold's-style gyms) — machine-and-membership businesses with corporate polish but no soul, no founder, no dance DNA.
2. **Small local dance/Zumba operators** (Pavans Dance to Fitness, G Nex, Fusion Dance & Zumba, PBS Fitness) — warm but digitally invisible, with weak or nonexistent websites.

Nobody in Nagpur occupies the position 2Stepz already owns in real life: **the boutique, women-first, celebration-of-movement studio led by a recognizable fitness personality.** The website's job is to take a position that already exists offline and make it undeniable online.

## The recommendation in one paragraph

Build a **mobile-first, boutique-premium, founder-forward website** whose emotional register is *joyful energy elevated by craft* — closer to Barry's warmth than Equinox's coldness, localized with Indian color and Power-Garba pride. Structure every page around three conversion paths (WhatsApp chat, tap-to-call, book-a-trial form), anchor trust in the founder's titles + a decade of operation + real reviews, and build the SEO architecture to own the "Zumba / dance fitness / women's fitness + Nagpur" keyword cluster that no local competitor is seriously contesting.

## What success looks like

- **90 days:** Website live, Google Business Profile fully rebuilt and linked, WhatsApp click-to-chat wired into every page, ranking movement on "zumba classes nagpur."
- **6 months:** Top-3 map pack for the core Zumba/dance-fitness cluster in West Nagpur; trial bookings arriving weekly from organic search; review count growing on Google (not just Justdial).
- **12 months:** The default answer to "best dance fitness studio in Nagpur" — in search results, in Instagram tags, and in word of mouth that now has a link to forward.

Before you ask

Honest answers.

I’ve never danced. I have two left feet.

Perfect — half the room started exactly that way. There are no “wrong” steps here, and Bokwa was practically invented for you. By week two you’ll wonder what you were worried about.

Am I too old or too unfit to start?

Our members range from school kids to grandmothers. Every format scales to your pace, and instructors give low-impact options in every single class.

Is it ladies-only?

Most batches are predominantly women and the studio is proudly women-first. Message us and we’ll match you to the batch where you’ll feel most at home.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, sports shoes and a water bottle. We’ll take care of the music, the energy and the dandiya sticks.

How do fees work?

Plans run monthly, quarterly or annually and always include all group formats. Ping us on WhatsApp for the current offer — festival seasons usually bring a good one.

Where exactly are you?

Second floor, Gubre Bhavan, Plot 63, Canal Road, Gokulpeth — two minutes from Times of India square. Take the stairs up; the music will guide you from there.
